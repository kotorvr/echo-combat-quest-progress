What looks different?

    Spot the difference

    The same view on the PC version and on the Quest 3. If something on the Quest looks different from the PC, circle it and say what it is: every note becomes an item on the fix list.

    Drag the divider to compare, or switch to Side by side. Press Mark a difference, drag on the picture to draw a red circle (as many as you need) and write a short note; a note without a circle is fine too. Then press Send note. Copy report gives the same text to paste where the project is discussed, if sending does not work.

    A sent note goes to the project's own notes service and shows on this page for every visitor: the view, your circles, your text and the day, nothing else. No account and no name; write nothing personal. Until you press Send note, your circles stay in this browser only.

    Arrows (or the arrow keys) step through the views of one map; the names at the top switch the map.
