Spot the difference

The same view on the PC version and on the Quest 3. If something on the Quest looks different from the PC, circle it and say what it is: every note becomes an item on the fix list.

Drag the divider to compare, or switch to Side by side. Press Mark a difference, drag on the picture to draw a red circle (as many as you need) and write a short note; a note without a circle is fine too. Then press Copy report and paste it where the project is discussed.

Nothing leaves this page until you copy it. Your circles are kept in this browser only.
